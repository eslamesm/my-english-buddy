## Cross-grade acceptance gates

- Meaning choices do not stand in for listening, oral production, handwriting, projects or actual visual recognition.
- Human listening is required before isolated phoneme/blending models are called ready. Prepare reviewable samples separately; do not silently waive this gate.
- Grammar needs explicit teaching, controlled practice and contextual use, not only word presence. Maintain grade and source provenance.
- Every new interaction needs accessible alternatives, transparent practice labels, independent keys, narrow/wide pixel checks, offline checks and unchanged progression/history checks.
- Review and merge checks precede GitHub or production changes. No release authorization is implied by this plan.
