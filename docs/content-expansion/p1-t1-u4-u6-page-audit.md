## Original content decisions and cautions

- These are page-audited objectives, not implemented unit completion. All units still need original interactive tasks, instruction, practice, and measured outcomes across listening, meaning, responses and formation. Text choice checks alone cover meaning practice only.
- Quantities and body-part forms must follow each task's depicted person/object. Do not imply every learner has a particular body part or ability. Use an imaginary character and optional participation.
- Sense activities are language examples, not a medical claim that every child sees/hears or must use a specific sense. Multiple senses can apply to a real object, so new checks need a stated scenario rather than an ambiguous flower/sun picture alone.
- Farm/jungle/pet groupings are book-context categories, not universal habitat or ownership rules. For example, a cat may live on a farm, and a lion is not a universal jungle-habitat example. Original checks must supply context, not teach an exclusive biological category.
- Big/small are relative. Original comparisons must identify the objects being compared rather than treating size as an absolute animal property.
- U2 shape ambiguity remains. Glossary127 describes circle as a round shape without corners and rectangle/square through sides/angles; keep circle2D distinct from sphere3D and avoid claiming every rectangle has unequal adjacent sides.
- Whole-word initial letters do not establish one universally identical phoneme or prove accurate speech. Human listening remains required before new isolated sound/blending clips.
- The book includes food/animal-product outcomes in U5 overview and Review2, but the intervening unit pages principally show animals, sizes and numbers. Do not invent unobserved dedicated lessons to bridge this difference. Verify teacher guide/assessment materials separately.
- No book pages, illustrations, dialogue or official audio copied into app assets.
