## Counting rules

- This table contains 16 audited pages, not 16 objectives or 16 completed lessons.
- Repeated words and repeated practice are not separate new objectives by default.
- Story language may exceed the unit's explicit assessed target. It is recorded as observed exposure, not automatically as a grammar mastery requirement.
- A support-course overlap does not become official unit coverage without a specific activity, evidence and tested grade assignment.
- The current pilot's six grammar lessons remain independent supports. The first-person sentence overlaps the unit, but this is not a complete unit grammar claim.
- No source book pages, art, story dialogue or official audio are included in application assets.

## Remaining gates

The unit audit identifies missing contextual, tracing, phonics and integrated activities. It does not close those gaps. Human listening review is still required before isolated T and short-I models are accepted. Grade-specific activities must remain separate from the existing vocabulary-based grade transition gate until their policy is deliberately decided and tested.
