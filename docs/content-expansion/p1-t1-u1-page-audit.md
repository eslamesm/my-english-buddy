## Scope boundary

- Six general grammar supports do not equal the P1 grammar syllabus. Unit 1 particularly needs first-person self-introduction and commands, taught in context.
- Article/demonstrative/plural supports may help later units, but are not assigned to this unit based on incidental overlap.
- Images, source pages and book dialogue are not application assets.
- Official audio, teacher guidance and full-year objective counts remain unverified.
