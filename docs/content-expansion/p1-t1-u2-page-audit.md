## Decisions and limits

- 17 pages audited. No new application byte was changed for this audit. Existing P1 context lessons still cover selected Unit 1 meanings only.
- Unit 2 expects responses and short descriptive phrases in addition to word labels. Independent be/articles/demonstratives supports are not automatically this unit's grammar coverage.
- Shapes must be geometrically unambiguous in new work. The page36 blue shape has near-equal sides despite the rectangle label; record the mismatch rather than copying it as a teaching key. A square is a rectangle mathematically, but a young-child contrast task should not rely on that unstated hierarchy.
- Colors in the book are examples, not universal facts about real objects. Original tasks should state the depicted or imagined object's color explicitly.
- No book illustrations, dialogue, full pages or official audio were copied into app assets. Official audio/teacher-guide/assessment verification remains open.
