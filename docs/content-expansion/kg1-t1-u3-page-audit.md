## Decisions and limits

- 26 pages audited, not 26 objectives or completed lessons.
- The earlier scope-only concern about head is resolved: it occurs in the story on PDF page 64, printed page 60. Keep the baseline head entry. Scope-table absence did not mean whole-unit absence.
- The baseline includes hair, face, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, teeth, arms, hands, legs and feet. Source pages also use singular arm, hand, foot, eye and ear. Concept overlap does not prove exact word-form coverage or contextual listening, and there is no automatic migration of existing words/history/gates.
- Hygiene actions and full phrases are not covered by the body-part word list. Independent support grammar does not close this unit gap without an assigned, tested unit activity.
- Story-only forms are recorded as exposure, not automatically made new grammar mastery requirements.
- No source book pages, art, dialogue or official audio were copied into application assets. This is a source audit, not a unit implementation or official endorsement.
