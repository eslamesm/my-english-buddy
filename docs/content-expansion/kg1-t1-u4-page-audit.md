## Decisions and limits

- 26 Unit 4 pages and six end-review/project pages inspected, plus final non-instructional pages. All four KG1 Term 1 unit page ranges are now audited. This does not mean their activities are implemented, nor that all preliminary pages or official assessments have been reviewed.
- The scope-table function list is a planning lead. The audited story provides some food preference/affection exposure, not evidence of a complete explicit likes/dislikes lesson or assessment.
- The shared written letter E does not make egg and eagle a same-initial-sound pair. Any original phonics work must distinguish letter identity from sound, with human listening review before isolated models.
- Family-tree tasks should permit a child's own family or an imagined family, without requiring disclosure of private family details or assigning a score to family structure.
- Existing baseline vocabulary/history/gates remain untouched. Whole-word overlap is not exact Ministry unit coverage. No book artwork, full pages, dialogues or official audio were copied into application assets.
