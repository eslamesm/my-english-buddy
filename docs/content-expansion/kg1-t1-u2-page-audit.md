## Decisions and limits

- 19 pages audited, not 19 objectives or completed lessons.
- The current book's desk target is confirmed on two unit pages, not merely inferred from the scope table. Preserve legacy table/history and the existing grade gate until an explicit migration policy is reviewed. Add the source gap to mapping rather than silently replacing the older vocabulary.
- Independent support grammar and sound-awareness overlap does not close unit coverage without a grade-linked activity and evidence.
- No source book pages, art, story dialogue or official audio have been copied into application assets.
- Unit 2 remains unimplemented as a unit. Tracing, integrated quantities/colors/shapes, story, project and oral/listening performance remain distinct gaps.
